Ad service privacy

LBRY Community is an independent Android fork. This service delivers reviewed sponsor banners and records aggregate campaign performance.

App reports

Short-lived random delivery receipts let us count banner impressions and confirmed outbound clicks. Requests do not include your LBRY account, wallet, viewing history or a persistent advertising identifier. Daily aggregate counts are retained for reporting. They are not unique-user counts or verified website visits.

Video likes

Likes are optional and do not require sign-in. The app keeps a random private installation secret and derives a different token for each video. When you open a video, its public claim ID and that video-specific token are sent to retrieve the count and your like state. When you like it, the server stores the claim ID and a hash of that token; unliking removes the record. No wallet or LBRY account is sent. Counts belong to this fork and are not Odysee counts. Reinstalling or clearing app data loses your ability to remove previous likes and may let you like again; these are not verified unique-person counts. Tokens differ across videos, but connections still expose IP addresses.

Connections and sign-in

DigitalOcean hosts this service. Connections necessarily expose an IP address to the server and hosting provider. Routine web access logs are disabled. Web error logs may contain connection details and are rotated daily, retaining seven rotations. Failed dashboard login IP addresses are retained for up to 15 minutes for throttling. Owner sessions last up to eight hours and use a secure, HTTP-only cookie. Server and hosting security records may have separate retention.

Campaigns and backups

Artwork, payment references, owner decisions and aggregate reports are private to the owner except for approved live banner details. Payment confirmation is manual; this service does not receive wallet keys or verify blockchain payments. Private database backups and daily server backups support recovery. Reports are shared by the owner.

External websites

Visiting a sponsor website is optional and requires confirmation in the app. That website has its own privacy practices.
